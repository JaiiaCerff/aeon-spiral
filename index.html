What the picture means, and which parts are theorems.

The object

An aeon is a curve (τ, S): a clock τ and an entropy S. Nothing else is assumed. The clock becomes height h ∈ [0,1]; the entropy becomes a phase

θ = 2π (S − S₀) / S_turn

so a full turn is a fixed amount of entropy. The local tightness of the spiral is the entropy production rate in that clock, dS/dh. For an equilibrium system cooled by expansion, with T ∝ 1/a,

dS / d ln(1/T) = −T dS/dT = −C

the winding density is the heat capacity, and a second-order critical point is where the spiral winds infinitely tightly.

Conformal lenses

Stereographic projection of the sphere to the plane and the Mercator map to the strip are both conformal (angle-preserving):

ρ = tan(π/4 + φ/2) = e^y, y = ln tan(π/4 + φ/2) = gd⁻¹(φ)

A curve of constant bearing β to the meridians is a straight line θ = y·tan β + c on the strip, a logarithmic spiral ρ = e^{(θ−c)/tan β} on the plane, and a loxodrome on the sphere. Constant bearing means S grows by a fixed amount per e-fold of the clock: a scale-free law, mapped to itself by any rescaling. The bearing is the invariant every conformal lens agrees on; it is reported at the cursor.

The background universe

H² = H₀² (Ω_r a⁻⁴ + Ω_m a⁻³ + Ω_Λ), t = ∫ da/(aH), η = ∫ da/(a²H)

With Λ > 0 the conformal time η converges: the infinite future is a finite boundary, ℐ⁺, which is what lets Penrose treat it as a surface to glue. Comoving entropy of radiation is conserved (g_s T³ a³ = const), so all growth of the universe's entropy is gravitational: black holes, with S = k_B A / 4ℓ_P², up to the de Sitter bound

S_dS = π c⁵ / (ħ G H_∞²) ≈ 10^122.5 k_B

Conformal cyclic cosmology

Rescale the metric, ĝ = Ω² g. Null cones, and the equations of massless fields, do not notice. A universe containing only massless quanta has no clocks or rulers, so its far future is indistinguishable, except by scale, from a radiation-dominated Big Bang whose Weyl curvature vanishes. Penrose identifies ℐ⁺ of one aeon with the Big Bang of the next, with Ω → ∞ on one side and Ω → 0 on the other (the reciprocal hypothesis ω = −1/Ω). On the sphere this is the north pole of one aeon being the south pole of the next; on the plane it is the inversion z → 1/z; both poles have the same shadow on the floor and differ only by level. On the covering space (the helicoid) the aeon index is the sheet number, and the second law is the statement that the level, not the position, is monotone, unless information lost into black holes is discounted, which is Penrose's reset.

Thresholds

Ising: T_c = 2J / ln(1+√2), C ∼ −ln|T−T_c|, m = [1 − sinh⁻⁴(2βJ)]^{1/8} Logistic: h_KS = λ⁺ (Pesin), r_∞ = 3.5699456…, λ(4) = ln 2 Kuramoto: K_c = 2/(π g(0)) = 2γ, r = √(1 − K_c/K) Percolation: p_c = 0.59274605 (site, square), ν = 4/3, β = 5/36

Each is an emergence threshold: an order parameter that is exactly zero on one side and non-zero on the other, with a diverging correlation length or response at the boundary. Three of them concern time directly: the KS entropy rate is the birth of an arrow of time in a reversible rule; the Kuramoto order parameter is a collective clock that exists only above K_c; and at a critical point the system is conformally invariant, the same symmetry CCC uses at the boundary of an aeon.
